# Console design system

Dark technical surfaces, clear status colors, dense evidence inspectors, and strong typography fit Deadwire’s operational purpose. Avoid generic SaaS card grids. The incident boundary, recovery state, and evidence gaps must be visible before secondary metadata.

Use amber for manual/unsafe caution, blue for analysis, green for verified/clean recovery, and red only for active recurrence or halted batches. Honor reduced-motion preferences in timelines and the animated presentation.
